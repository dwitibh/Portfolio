I'm Dwiti, a Senior Data Analyst and BI Engineer based in Dublin. Over 10+ years across finance, facilities management, and tech, the through line has always been the same: take data nobody fully trusts, make it reliable, and turn it into something a decision maker actually uses.

Most recently at Mitie, a FTSE 250 facilities business, I built the award-winning NPS Intelligence Platform used by 1,000+ people, a revenue risk model protecting £5M annually, and commercial analytics across an £800M+ contract portfolio. I also led the migration of marketing analytics from Supermetrics onto Microsoft Fabric, owning the full chain: pipelines, medallion architecture, semantic models, and the final report.

What keeps me in this field is the moment a dashboard changes a decision. I care about the engineering underneath as much as the visual on top, which is why I certified as a Fabric Analytics Engineer (DP-600) and why I build data science projects end to end in my own time: ML models, time series forecasting, and AI-driven workflow automation with n8n. I also write about practical analytics on Substack, with a practitioner's voice rather than a thought leader's.
